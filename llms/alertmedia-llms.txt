# AlertMedia

> AlertMedia is a unified risk intelligence and response platform that helps organizations keep their people safe, informed, and connected before, during, and after critical events. It brings together real-time, analyst-verified risk intelligence, mass notification, employee safety monitoring, travel risk management, social media intelligence, and incident management and response in one system.

## Platform capabilities

- [Real-Time Signals](https://www.alertmedia.com/platform/real-time-signals/): AI-powered monitoring that helps teams surface potential threats faster.
- [Visual Intelligence](https://www.alertmedia.com/platform/visual-intelligence/): Geospatial context, visual data, and risk information for faster analysis and response.
- [Analytics](https://www.alertmedia.com/platform/analytics/): Reporting for notification delivery, engagement, reach, and response performance.
- [AI Assistant](https://www.alertmedia.com/platform/ai-assistant/): AI-assisted message drafting, translation, and communication support during critical events.
